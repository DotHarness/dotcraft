# Interactive answers

When a calculator, a comparison, or a quiz answers a question better than a paragraph, DotCraft puts one right in its reply. Adjust it while you read, and use its buttons to ask a follow-up or copy the result. Desktop draws it with the same controls as the rest of the app.

## Make everyday answers clearer

A plain answer is a snapshot: one more guest, a different hotel, or a heavier month of data, and you have to ask again. An interactive answer keeps the inputs in the reply, so you change them yourself and the result follows. Switch between the text answer and the interactive one to compare.

<LiveExample feature="generative-ui" compare :examples="['dinner', 'plans', 'trip']" :height="800" />

The reply still states the answer in its text. Copying the message, reading it in another client, or seeing it in a notification keeps the conclusion.

## Ask for a tool

Ask for the tool itself and DotCraft builds it on the spot: a loan calculator, a unit converter, or a quick quiz before an exam.

## Get one when you want it

DotCraft decides on its own when interaction helps, so usually you just ask your question. To be sure you get one, say what you want to change or do, such as "Give me a calculator where I can change the rate and the term" or "Quiz me with three questions."

Interactive answers appear in Desktop. Other entry points, such as the CLI or a chat bot, show the text of the same reply.

## Work with the answer

Sliders, fields, and choices update the result immediately, and your changes are still there when you reopen the conversation. Buttons can send a follow-up message, fill in the message box for you to finish, copy a result, or open a link after you confirm it. The controls switch on once DotCraft finishes writing the reply.

For free-form graphics, simulations, and design mockups, DotCraft draws a [visualization](./visualizations) instead.
